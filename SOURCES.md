# Asset provenance and content review

## Original work

The Arch Arena name treatment, geometric mark, layout, interface copy, architectural hero linework, and question diagrams were created for this project. They do not reproduce Seenjeem branding or assets.

The reference website was inspected for the underlying experience: category selection, point-value questions, team competition, and limited-use assistance. Its logo, illustrations, question text, and visual layout were not used.

Original schematic assets:

- `plan.svg`: diagrammatic floor plan, not a construction document.
- `section.svg`: diagrammatic vertical building section.
- `elevation.svg`: diagrammatic orthographic elevation.
- `truss.svg`: idealized structural-system identification illustration; not an engineered truss specification.
- `brick.svg`: simplified running-bond pattern.
- `burj-sketch.svg`: conceptual landmark silhouette, not a measured building elevation.

Copies named `q-v00` through `q-v08` are used for questions so that filenames do not give away the answer. Photographic question copies are re-encoded without the source metadata.

## Question content

Questions and explanations were authored as editorial starter content for this application. Source fields identify review context; they are not a representation that each answer has been independently verified against a primary source during this build.

Before the event, organizers should verify the bank against their teaching material and authoritative references, especially for project attribution, structural claims, dates, regional terminology, and climate-dependent design principles. Useful reference starting points include:

- Pritzker Architecture Prize biographies: https://www.pritzkerprize.com/
- UNESCO World Heritage Centre: https://whc.unesco.org/
- Louvre Abu Dhabi: https://www.louvreabudhabi.ae/
- Museum of the Future: https://museumofthefuture.ae/
- Foster + Partners project archive: https://www.fosterandpartners.com/
- buildingSMART for IFC: https://www.buildingsmart.org/
- Official building owners, architects’ project archives, and your department’s approved history, structures, environmental design, and drawing references.

No university founding dates, campus architects, or local event-specific facts have been invented. Add those questions using the question manager after confirming the details.
